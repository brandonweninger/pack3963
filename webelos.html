Webelos Den (4th Grade)

Webelos is one of the most exciting years in Cub Scouting. At Pack 3963, Webelos Scouts take on advanced adventures, learn real outdoor skills, and begin preparing for the transition to Arrow of Light and eventually Scouts BSA. This is the year when Scouts start becoming true leaders within the Pack.

Webelos stands for “WE’ll BE LOyal Scouts,” and the program focuses on responsibility, independence, and skill development. Webelos Scouts participate in more camping, more hands‑on activities, and more leadership opportunities than any previous rank.

What Webelos Looks Like at Pack 3963

  • Age: 4th Grade (typically 9–10 years old).
  • Focus: Leadership, camping, outdoor skills, and responsibility.
  • Meeting Style: More structured, skill‑based, and outdoors whenever possible.
  • Pack Events: Webelos participate in campouts, service projects, Pinewood Derby, and leadership roles.
  • Skill Development: Knots, first aid, fire building, cooking, hiking, and teamwork.

Webelos Scouts begin learning skills that prepare them for Scouts BSA, including camping basics, leadership roles, and outdoor safety.

Webelos Adventures

Webelos adventures are more advanced and help Scouts build confidence, leadership, and outdoor skills. Completing required adventures and electives earns the Webelos rank.

Examples of Webelos Adventures

  • Cast Iron Chef: Outdoor cooking, meal planning, and fire safety.
  • First Responder: Basic first aid, emergency preparedness, and safety skills.
  • Stronger, Faster, Higher: Fitness challenges and personal goal setting.
  • Webelos Walkabout: Hiking skills, map reading, and outdoor awareness.
  • Engineer: Hands‑on building and problem‑solving activities.

Parent & Guardian Role

Webelos Scouts are becoming more independent, but parent involvement is still important. At Pack 3963, we ask that parents:

  • Support at‑home adventure requirements.
  • Attend outings and campouts when possible.
  • Encourage leadership and responsibility.
  • Communicate with den leaders about scheduling and needs.

Joining the Webelos Den

If your child is entering 4th grade and you live in Jefferson Park, Portage Park, or the Northwest Side of Chicago, they are eligible to join the Webelos den at Pack 3963.
