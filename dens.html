Our Dens

Cub Scout Pack 3963 is organized into dens by grade level. Each den focuses on age‑appropriate adventures, skills, and activities that help Scouts grow in confidence, character, and leadership.

Click on a rank below to learn more about what that den does at Pack 3963, including adventures, meeting expectations, and how families can support their Scouts.

Den Overview

  • Lion (Kindergarten) – Introductory Cub Scouting with parent‑led activities.
  • Tiger (1st Grade) – Shared leadership between parents and leaders, fun entry‑level adventures.
  • Wolf (2nd Grade) – Growing independence, outdoor skills, and teamwork.
  • Bear (3rd Grade) – Hands‑on projects, outdoor cooking, and community service.
  • Webelos (4th Grade) – Advanced adventures, camping skills, and leadership development.
  • Arrow of Light (5th Grade) – Transition to Scouts BSA, leadership projects, and preparation for the next level.

Not sure which den your child belongs in? Cub Scouts is organized by grade level. If you have questions, visit our Contact Page or email cubscouts3963@gmail.com.
