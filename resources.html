Uniform Guide

Below is the standard uniform for each Cub Scout rank in Pack 3963. These items follow the official BSA uniform guidelines and match what Scouts wear at pack and den meetings.

  • Lion (Kindergarten)
    • Lion T‑shirt
    • Lion cap (optional)
    • Optional uniform bottoms (shorts, pants, skort)
  • Tiger (1st Grade)
    • Blue Cub Scout uniform shirt
    • Tiger neckerchief
    • Tiger neckerchief slide
    • Official Cub Scout belt (for adventure loops)
  • Wolf (2nd Grade)
    • Blue Cub Scout uniform shirt
    • Wolf neckerchief
    • Wolf neckerchief slide
    • Official Cub Scout belt
  • Bear (3rd Grade)
    • Blue Cub Scout uniform shirt
    • Bear neckerchief
    • Bear neckerchief slide
    • Official Cub Scout belt
  • Webelos (4th Grade)
    • Tan Scouts BSA uniform shirt
    • Webelos neckerchief
    • Webelos neckerchief slide
    • Optional: Webelos Colors (for adventure pins)
  • Arrow of Light (5th Grade)
    • Tan Scouts BSA uniform shirt
    • AOL neckerchief
    • AOL neckerchief slide
    • Optional: Webelos Colors
