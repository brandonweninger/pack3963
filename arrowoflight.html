Arrow of Light (5th Grade)

Arrow of Light is the highest rank in Cub Scouts and the final step before entering Scouts BSA. At Pack 3963, AOL Scouts take on advanced outdoor skills, leadership responsibilities, and teamwork challenges that prepare them for the next level of Scouting.

AOL Scouts are the senior youth leaders of the Pack. They serve as role models for younger Scouts, participate in more challenging adventures, and begin working with local Scouts BSA troops to explore the next stage of their Scouting journey.

What Arrow of Light Looks Like at Pack 3963

  • Age: 5th Grade (typically 10–11 years old).
  • Focus: Leadership, camping, outdoor skills, and transition to Scouts BSA.
  • Meeting Style: Skill‑based, outdoors whenever possible, and leadership‑focused.
  • Pack Events: AOL Scouts participate in campouts, service projects, Pinewood Derby, and leadership roles.
  • Skill Development: Fire building, cooking, knots, first aid, hiking, and teamwork.

AOL Scouts begin learning skills that prepare them for Scouts BSA, including camping basics, leadership roles, and outdoor safety.

Arrow of Light Adventures

AOL adventures are the most advanced in Cub Scouting and help Scouts build confidence, leadership, and outdoor skills. Completing required adventures and electives earns the Arrow of Light rank.

Examples of Arrow of Light Adventures

  • Building a Better World: Citizenship, community service, and global awareness.
  • Outdoor Adventurer: Camping skills, fire building, and outdoor safety.
  • Scouting Adventure: Introduction to Scouts BSA skills and troop activities.
  • Duty to God in Action: Reflection, values, and personal growth.
  • Castaway: Survival skills, shelter building, and resourcefulness.

Parent & Guardian Role

AOL Scouts are becoming independent leaders, but parent involvement is still important. At Pack 3963, we ask that parents:

  • Support at‑home adventure requirements.
  • Attend outings and campouts when possible.
  • Encourage leadership and responsibility.
  • Communicate with den leaders about scheduling and needs.

Joining the Arrow of Light Den

If your child is entering 5th grade and you live in Jefferson Park, Portage Park, or the Northwest Side of Chicago, they are eligible to join the Arrow of Light den at Pack 3963.
