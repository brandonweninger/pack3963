Welcome to Cub Scout Pack 3963

Cub Scout Pack 3963 is a family-focused, adventure-driven Pack serving the Northwest Side of Chicago. We welcome boys and girls from kindergarten through fifth grade, offering outdoor adventures, hands-on learning, community service, and leadership development.

Our first Pack meeting of the 2026–2027 Scouting year will be held on September 19th at 9:30 AM at Eden United Church of Chicago, located at 5051 W Gunnison St, Chicago, IL 60630, in the Jefferson Park neighborhood.

This meeting will also serve as an Open House for new families interested in exploring Cub Scouts as an option for their children. Families are welcome to stop in, meet our leaders, learn about the program, and see what Pack 3963 is all about.

Our Pack meets monthly during the school year, with den meetings scheduled by each den leader. We serve families from across the Portage Park, Jefferson Park, and surrounding neighborhoods.
