Welcome to Cub Scout Pack 3963

Cub Scout Pack 3963 is a family-focused, adventure-driven Pack serving the Northwest Side of Chicago. We welcome boys and girls from kindergarten through fifth grade, offering:

  • Outdoor adventures and camping
  • Hands-on learning and STEM activities
  • Community service projects
  • Leadership development and teamwork

📅 First Pack Meeting & Open House

Date: September 19th, 2026

Time: 9:30 AM

Location: Eden United Church of Chicago

Address: 5051 W Gunnison St, Chicago, IL 60630

New families are invited to join us for an Open House to meet our leaders, learn about the program, and explore Cub Scouts as an option for their children.
