Welcome to Pack 3963!

Cub Scouting is all about fun with a purpose — building character, learning new skills, exploring the outdoors, and growing together as families. Pack 3963 meets regularly throughout the school year and offers exciting activities, service projects, and adventures for Scouts from kindergarten through fifth grade.

Our Dens

Pack 3963 includes Scouts from kindergarten through fifth grade, organized into dens by grade level.

Lion

Kindergarten Scouts starting their Cub Scouting journey.

Tiger

1st Grade Scouts exploring new skills and adventures.

Wolf

2nd Grade Scouts building confidence and teamwork.

Bear

3rd Grade Scouts taking on bigger challenges.

Webelos

4th Grade Scouts preparing for Scouts BSA.

Arrow of Light

5th Grade Scouts ready for the next step in Scouting.

About Pack 3963

Pack 3963 serves families in the Chicago area, focusing on outdoor adventure, hands-on learning, community service, family involvement, and leadership development.

We welcome all families interested in joining the Scouting journey and building lasting memories together.
