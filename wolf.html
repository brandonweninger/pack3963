Wolf Den (2nd Grade)

Wolf is an exciting year of Cub Scouting where Scouts begin showing real independence, responsibility, and teamwork. At Pack 3963, Wolves take on more challenging adventures, learn outdoor skills, and participate actively in Pack events. This is the year when Scouts start to feel like “big kids” in the program.

Den Leadership

Wolf leaders guide Scouts through increasingly hands‑on adventures, outdoor activities, and early leadership opportunities. Parents are encouraged to support at‑home requirements and attend outings.

What Wolf Looks Like at Pack 3963

  • Age: 2nd Grade (typically 7–8 years old).
  • Focus: Independence, outdoor skills, teamwork, and responsibility.
  • Meeting Style: More structured, with hands‑on projects and outdoor activities.
  • Pack Events: Wolves participate in campouts, service projects, Pinewood Derby, and more.
  • Skill Development: Basic first aid, knots, cooking, and nature exploration.

Wolves begin taking on more responsibility during meetings and outings. They learn how to follow instructions, work as a team, and complete tasks with growing independence.

Wolf Adventures

Wolf adventures help Scouts build confidence, learn practical skills, and explore the world around them. Completing required adventures and electives earns the Wolf rank.

Examples of Wolf Adventures

  • Call of the Wild: Outdoor skills, camping basics, and nature exploration.
  • Howling at the Moon: Communication, skits, and performing for others.
  • Running with the Pack: Fitness, games, and teamwork challenges.
  • Code of the Wolf: Problem‑solving, puzzles, and simple math‑based activities.
  • Adventures in Coins: Learning about money, value, and simple budgeting.

Parent & Guardian Role

Wolf Scouts are becoming more independent, but parent involvement is still important. At Pack 3963, we ask that parents:

  • Support at‑home adventure requirements.
  • Attend outings and campouts when possible.
  • Encourage responsibility and teamwork.
  • Communicate with den leaders about scheduling and needs.

Joining the Wolf Den

If your child is entering 2nd grade and you live in Jefferson Park, Portage Park, or the Northwest Side of Chicago, they are eligible to join the Wolf den at Pack 3963.
