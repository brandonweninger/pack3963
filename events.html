Upcoming Pack 3963 Events

Cub Scout Pack 3963 offers year-round activities for families across Jefferson Park, Portage Park, and the Northwest Side of Chicago. From camping trips to STEM nights, our events help Scouts learn, grow, and have fun.

Fall 2026 Events

September 19 – First Pack Meeting & Open House

Time: 9:30 AM

Location: Eden United Church of Chicago

Join us for our first meeting of the year! New families are welcome to meet our leaders, learn about Cub Scouts, and sign up for the 2026–2027 program year.

October 10–12 – Fall Camping Trip at White Pines

Time: Weekend Overnight Trip

Location: White Pines State Park, Illinois

Our annual Fall Camping Trip is one of the biggest adventures of the year! Scouts and families will enjoy hiking, campfire cooking, outdoor skills, nature exploration, and plenty of time together in one of Illinois’ most beautiful state parks. This is a perfect opportunity for new families to experience Cub Scout camping.

November 9 – STEM Discovery Night

Time: 6:00 PM

Location: Portage Park Fieldhouse

Hands-on science experiments, engineering challenges, and robotics demonstrations.

December 14 – Holiday Service Project

Time: 9:30 AM

Location: Irving Park United Methodist Church

Scouts and families will assemble care kits for local shelters and participate in a community holiday celebration.

Year-Round Activities

Pack 3963 participates in a variety of annual Cub Scout traditions:

  • Pinewood Derby
  • Raingutter Regatta
  • Camping trips
  • STEM nights
  • Community service projects
  • Outdoor skills workshops
  • Family hikes
