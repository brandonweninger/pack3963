Frequently Asked Questions

Cub Scouts is open to boys and girls in grades K–5. Pack 3963 includes Lion (K), Tiger (1st), Wolf (2nd), Bear (3rd), Webelos (4th), and Arrow of Light (5th).

We meet at Eden United Church of Christ, located at 5051 W Gunnison St, Chicago, IL 60630.

Registration fees are set by the Boy Scouts of America and include national dues, insurance, and Pack-level activity support. Financial assistance is available for families who need it.

Absolutely! Cub Scouting is powered by dedicated volunteers. Adults can register as leaders, committee members, or event helpers through the same BSA registration portal.

Meeting Schedule

Pack 3963 meets on Saturday mornings from 9:30 AM to 11:00 AM at Eden United Church of Christ. Additional events include camping trips, STEM activities, service projects, and family outings.
