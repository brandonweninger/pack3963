Tiger Den (1st Grade)

Tiger is the first full year of Cub Scouting. At Pack 3963, Tigers begin developing independence, teamwork, and confidence through fun, hands‑on adventures. Tigers learn by doing — exploring the outdoors, building simple projects, and discovering new skills with the support of their families and den leaders.

Tiger is also the year when Scouts begin practicing shared leadership. Families take turns helping with activities, games, or small parts of meetings, giving Tigers a chance to see adults model teamwork.

What Tiger Looks Like at Pack 3963

  • Age: 1st Grade (typically 6–7 years old).
  • Focus: Hands‑on learning, teamwork, and early outdoor skills.
  • Leadership: Families rotate helping with small parts of meetings.
  • Meeting Style: Active, project‑based, and outdoors whenever possible.
  • Pack Events: Tigers attend Pack meetings, campouts, service projects, and outings.

Tigers begin participating in more Pack‑wide activities than Lions, including camping, service projects, and outdoor adventures appropriate for their age.

Tiger Adventures

The Tiger rank is earned by completing required adventures and choosing electives that match the den’s interests. Adventures help Tigers build confidence, learn new skills, and explore their community.

Examples of Tiger Adventures

  • My Tiger Jungle: Exploring nature and learning about local wildlife.
  • Team Tiger: Practicing teamwork through games and simple challenges.
  • Tiger Bites: Learning about healthy eating and making simple snacks.
  • Safe & Smart: Understanding basic safety rules at home and outdoors.
  • Curiosity, Intrigue & Magical Mysteries: Fun elective adventure with simple magic tricks.

Parent & Guardian Role

Tiger is a shared‑leadership year. At Pack 3963, we ask that a parent or guardian:

  • Attend Tiger meetings with their Scout.
  • Help lead or assist with one meeting or activity during the year.
  • Support at‑home adventure requirements when needed.
  • Communicate with the Tiger den leader about schedules and needs.

This involvement helps Tigers grow in confidence and ensures meetings run smoothly and safely.

Joining the Tiger Den

If your child is entering 1st grade and you live in Jefferson Park, Portage Park, or the Northwest Side of Chicago, they are eligible to join the Tiger den at Pack 3963.

To get started, visit our Contact Page or email cubscouts3963@gmail.com. A Pack leader will help you with registration, fees, and uniform information.
