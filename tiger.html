Tiger Den (1st Grade)

Tiger is the first full year of Cub Scouting. At Pack 3963, Tigers begin developing independence, teamwork, and confidence through fun, hands‑on adventures. Tigers learn by doing — exploring the outdoors, building simple projects, and discovering new skills with the support of their families and den leaders.

What Tiger Looks Like at Pack 3963

  • Age: 1st Grade (typically 6–7 years old).
  • Focus: Hands‑on learning, teamwork, and early outdoor skills.
  • Leadership: Families rotate helping with small parts of meetings.
  • Meeting Style: Active, project‑based, and outdoors whenever possible.
  • Pack Events: Tigers attend Pack meetings, campouts, service projects, and outings.

Tiger Adventures

Examples of Tiger Adventures

  • My Tiger Jungle: Exploring nature and learning about local wildlife.
  • Team Tiger: Practicing teamwork through games and simple challenges.
  • Tiger Bites: Learning about healthy eating and making simple snacks.
  • Safe & Smart: Understanding basic safety rules at home and outdoors.
  • Curiosity, Intrigue & Magical Mysteries: Fun elective adventure with simple magic tricks.
