Pack 3963 Leadership Team

Cub Scout Pack 3963 is powered by a dedicated team of volunteers who work together to create a safe, fun, and meaningful Scouting experience for families across Jefferson Park, Portage Park, and the Northwest Side of Chicago.

Scouting succeeds because of parents. Every event, every den meeting, every campout — they all happen thanks to the support, encouragement, and involvement of families. Whether you volunteer in a leadership role, help at an event, or simply cheer your Scout on, you are a vital part of our Pack’s success.

Cubmaster

The Cubmaster is the primary leader of the Pack. They run Pack meetings, coordinate activities, support den leaders, and help ensure that every Scout has a fun and rewarding experience.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Leads monthly Pack meetings
  • Supports den leaders and families
  • Coordinates Pack-wide events and activities
  • Ensures program quality, safety, and fun

Assistant Cubmaster

The Assistant Cubmaster helps run Pack meetings, supports the Cubmaster, and steps in whenever additional leadership is needed.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Assists with Pack meetings and ceremonies
  • Helps coordinate Pack events
  • Supports den leaders and new families

Den Leaders

Den Leaders guide Scouts through rank-specific adventures, helping them learn new skills, explore the outdoors, and grow as leaders. Each den has its own leader and assistant leader.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Plan and run den meetings
  • Track advancement and achievements
  • Coordinate with Pack leadership
  • Support families and new Scouts

Committee Chair

The Committee Chair oversees Pack administration, supports leaders, and ensures the Pack operates smoothly behind the scenes.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Leads the Pack Committee
  • Manages Pack finances and records
  • Supports recruitment and communication
  • Ensures compliance with BSA policies

Pack Committee

The Pack Committee is a team of volunteers who handle logistics, communication, finances, advancement, and event support. They ensure the Pack runs smoothly and that leaders have everything they need to deliver a great program.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Event planning and coordination
  • Budgeting and fundraising
  • Record keeping and communication
  • Supporting leaders and families
