Contact Cub Scout Pack 3963

We’re excited to connect with new and returning families! Whether you have questions about joining, volunteering, den activities, or upcoming events, our leadership team is here to help.

Cub Scout Pack 3963 serves families across Jefferson Park, Portage Park, and the Northwest Side of Chicago. We look forward to hearing from you!

Pack 3963 meets at Eden United Church of Christ, located at 5051 W Gunnison St, Chicago, IL 60630.
